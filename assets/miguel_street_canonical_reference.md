## 章节原著关键线索与代表台词（Bilingual Excerpts）

### 第一章：博加特（Bogart）
- **原著开篇**：
  > *"Every morning when he got up Hat would sit on the banister of his back verandah and shout across, 'What happening there, Bogart?'*  
  > *Bogart would turn in his bed and mumble softly, so that no one heard, 'What happening there, Hat?'"*
- **原著内核**：门前挂着“裁缝”木牌，一台缝纫机、粉笔、几张单人纸牌。博加特终日躺在铁床上吃香蕉。他出走委内瑞拉后仿效亨弗莱·鲍嘉嘴衔香烟归来，最终因图纳普纳的重婚罪被捕，在手铐中找回了虚妄的“男子气概”。

### 第二章：叫不出名堂的事（The Thing Without a Name）
- **原著开篇**：
  > *"Popo was a carpenter, and he was proud of it. But Popo never made any furniture. Popo was always making 'the thing without a name.'*  
  > *'What you making, Popo?' I would ask.*  
  > *'Ha, boy! That's the question,' Popo would say."*
- **原著内核**：金色的刨花与木屑，未命名的形体。妻子离去后他发狂偷盗改装家具，入狱服刑归来被街坊称赞为“真汉子”，但他漆上俗艳红绿油漆，做起了量产桌椅，再也不做没有名字的东西了。

### 第四章：自择的职业（His Chosen Calling）
- **原著开篇**：
  > *"We were very proud of Elias. Elias was going to be a doctor. Titus Hoyt was coaching him... Titus Hoyt said, 'Elias, you have to be a doctor. You have to pass the Cambridge Senior.'"*
- **原著内核**：八次剑桥试屡败屡战，转考卫生检查员再度落空。最终他驾驭着两头骡子拉着的市政垃圾车，高傲地宣称：“这是我自择的崇高事业。”

### 第六章：B.华兹华斯（B. Wordsworth）
- **原著精髓**：
  > *"I am a poet. My name is B. Wordsworth. Black Wordsworth. White Wordsworth was my brother... I am writing the greatest poem in the world. One line a month. The first line is: The past is deep."*
- **原著内核**：萨瓦纳草坪看星空、吃芒果、纯洁的忘年之交。临终前用谎言击碎神话以保护少年的未来。小院被推土机铲平，一座洋房矗立——“就好像B.华兹华斯从未存在过一样”。

### 第十七章：我怎样离开米格尔街（How I Left Miguel Street）
- **原著精髓**：
  > *"I was eighteen. I had begun drinking with the men... My mother said, 'If you stay here, you will become like Bogart, like Popo, like all of them.'*  
  > *At Piarco, I looked down at the tarmac and watched my shadow. I didn't look back."*
